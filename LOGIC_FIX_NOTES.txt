UI/XAML: KHONG THAY DOI.

Logic da sua:
1. Them BoardIoDecoder de giai ma RX A0/A1/A2/A3 va map ve I/O toan cuc.
2. Tuong thich ca hai kieu firmware:
   - A0 + index 00..FF (kieu cu, IO 1..256)
   - A0/A1/A2/A3 + local index 00..7F (128 IO/card)
3. StartCardNumber va CardCount tu production.settings.json duoc dung cho mapping RX.
4. PinProbe khong con doc I/O tu chuoi Logs; nhan truc tiep ScanFrame tu board.
5. PinProbe tra nguoc I/O vao model de hien Connector / Pin / Wire va trang thai da map/chua map.
6. RX log chi ghi khi tap I/O thay doi, tranh nghen UI.
7. Giu nguyen command TX da co tu trace: 8C 00 02 00. Khong tu y thay protocol gui xuong board.
